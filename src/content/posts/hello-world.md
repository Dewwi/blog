I'm David Lequin, a data person in London who spent the last decade-plus wrangling pipelines, warehouses and the occasional 3am page. Lately I've been wrangling AI agents instead, and discovering that they fail in familiar ways, only with more personality.

## Why I'm writing

Getting an agent to do something impressive takes an afternoon. Getting it to do that same thing reliably, cheaply and observably, for real users, on a Tuesday, is a different job entirely. Most of what's written about agents covers the afternoon. I want to write about the Tuesday.

## What to expect

- **Agent infrastructure**, including running agents on Kubernetes
- **Evals and observability**: how you know the thing works, and notice when it stops
- **Data engineering for AI**: the plumbing that decides whether any of it is useful
- **Production war stories**: what worked, what didn't, and what caught fire

I build most of this with Claude, so expect honest notes on that too, including the parts that didn't go to plan.
